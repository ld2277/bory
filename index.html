Eggplant and Lentil Moussaka finally gets cooked — it has sat in the library since August, was unblocked when russet potatoes were priced on Nov 9, and was passed over last week for the jambalaya. It is the right dish for a cold week: an oven bake of roasted eggplant and potato over a cinnamon-spiced lentil sauce under a béchamel, with russets, storage onions and garlic all at their Northeast November best. Around it sit Cinnamon Roll Overnight Oats, the longest-rested breakfast on the board at six weeks, and a Huevos Rancheros Grain Bowl. The three share nothing: rolled oats against brown rice against a potato layer, Greek yogurt against eggs and black beans (Phaseolus vulgaris) against brown lentils (Lens culinaris), American against Mexican against Greek. Meals supply 88g of protein against the 81g the rules ask for, plus the 40g shake and about 19g of Greek yogurt with dinner — 147g a day. No expensive-aisle item at all this week, which is the shape that has bought the widest margins on record: recipe ingredients land at $50.98 against the $55 ceiling, $4.02 clear, with nothing cut to make budget. Prep order
  1. The night before: soak the black beans in plenty of cold water. It is the week's only overnight step and costs counter space, not time.
  2. Rice cooker on first with the brown rice. Drain the beans and set them boiling with half an onion and two garlic cloves — 45 to 55 minutes, unattended.
  3. Oven to 400°F (200°C). Slice, oil and salt the eggplant and potatoes and roast them on two trays for 25 minutes while the lentil sauce simmers on the stovetop.
  4. Make the béchamel, layer the moussaka and bake it 35 to 40 minutes. In that window boil and cool the eggs, build the six oat jars and pack the six bowls. Total active time: about 1h50.

Cinnamon Roll Overnight Oats

6 servings10 min active29g protein5g fibreAmerican

Ingredients

  • Rolled oats, 1 3/4 cups (300g)
  • Plain Greek yogurt, 3 cups (720g)
  • Protein powder, 3 scoops (90g) — from the bulk tub, not on the grocery list
  • Water, 2 cups (480ml)
  • Light brown sugar, 1/2 cup packed (100g)
  • Ground cinnamon, 2 tsp
  • Salt, a generous pinch

Method

  1. Whisk the protein powder into the water in a jug first, until no lumps remain. This is step one every oats week, because powder stirred straight into yogurt clumps and will not come back out.
  2. Stir the Greek yogurt into the thinned powder, then fold in the oats, brown sugar, cinnamon and salt until everything is evenly wet.
  3. Divide between six jars, seal them and refrigerate overnight. They are ready from the next morning and need nothing further.

29g protein and 5g fibre a serving. Holds the full six days in the fridge and is eaten cold straight from the jar — no day-of step at all. The cinnamon deepens over the week rather than fading. Brown sugar rather than honey is deliberate: honey counts against the one expensive-aisle item a week even when it is already in the cupboard, and this week's slot is better left empty.

Huevos Rancheros Grain Bowl

6 servings35 min active26g protein12g fibreMexican

Ingredients

  • Eggs, large, 12
  • Brown rice, 1 3/4 cups dry (330g)
  • Black beans, dried, 1 1/4 cups (240g)
  • Crushed tomatoes, 2 cans, 15 oz (425g) each
  • Poblano peppers, 2
  • Yellow onion, 2 medium
  • Garlic, 4 cloves
  • Jalapeños, 2, seeds in
  • Cilantro, 1 bunch
  • Lemon, 1
  • Vegetable oil, 3 tbsp (45ml)
  • Chili powder, 1 tbsp; cumin, 2 tsp; cayenne, 2 tsp; smoked paprika, 1 tsp; dried oregano, 1 tsp; salt

Method

  1. Soak the black beans overnight in plenty of cold water. The next day, drain them and boil them with half an onion and two whole garlic cloves for 45 to 55 minutes, until tender but not collapsing, then salt them and drain.
  2. Put the brown rice in the rice cooker with its usual water and leave it. It needs no attention and will be done long before anything else.
  3. Dice the remaining onion and the poblanos and fry them in the oil over a good heat until the edges catch, about 8 minutes. Add the chopped garlic and the jalapeños with their seeds and fry one minute more.
  4. Add the chili powder, cumin, cayenne, smoked paprika and oregano, stir for thirty seconds in the hot oil, then pour in both cans of crushed tomatoes. Simmer 15 to 20 minutes until it is a thick salsa roja rather than a sauce, and salt it well. No blender needed.
  5. Boil the eggs for 9 minutes, cool them under cold water and leave them in their shells.
  6. Pack six containers with rice, then beans, then salsa roja. Chop the cilantro and fold it through the salsa on prep day. Keep the eggs together in their shells in a separate box with the lemon.

26g protein and 12g fibre a serving. Holds six days; rice, beans and salsa microwave together in 2 minutes. One day-of step, and it is the only one in the week: peel two eggs, halve them onto the hot bowl and squeeze over the lemon. Peeled eggs keep only about four days, which is why they stay in the shell. The heat is two whole jalapeños and 2 tsp of cayenne and it will climb noticeably by day five.

Eggplant and Lentil Moussaka Bake

6 servings1h05 active33g protein31g fibreGreek

Ingredients

  • Brown lentils, dried, 2 1/4 cups (450g)
  • Purple eggplant, 3 medium, about 3 lb (1.4kg)
  • Russet potatoes, about 1.5 lb (700g)
  • Crushed tomatoes, 2 cans, 15 oz (425g) each
  • Yellow onion, 2 medium
  • Garlic, 4 cloves
  • Whole milk, 2 3/4 cups (650ml)
  • All-purpose flour, 1/3 cup (45g)
  • Vegetable oil, 1/2 cup (120ml)
  • Grated parmesan, 3/4 cup (80g)
  • Ground cinnamon, 1 tsp; dried oregano, 2 tsp; red pepper flakes, 2 tsp; cayenne, 1 1/2 tsp; black pepper; salt

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 400°F (200°C). Slice the eggplant into rounds about half an inch thick and the potatoes a little thinner. Brush both with oil, salt them and roast on two trays for 25 minutes, until the eggplant is collapsing and the potato is cooked through. Swap the trays halfway if your oven runs uneven.
  2. While they roast, dice the onions and fry them in 3 tablespoons of the oil until soft and just browning, about 10 minutes. Add the chopped garlic, the cinnamon, oregano, red pepper flakes and cayenne, and stir for a minute.
  3. Add the rinsed lentils and both cans of crushed tomatoes with a cup of water. Simmer 30 minutes, stirring now and then, until the lentils are tender and the sauce is thick enough to hold a spoon upright. Salt and pepper it hard — this layer carries the whole dish.
  4. Make the béchamel: heat 4 tablespoons of oil in a saucepan, whisk in the flour and cook it for a minute, then add the milk a splash at a time, whisking, until it is smooth and thick. Season it well.
  5. Layer a 9x13 dish: potato slices on the bottom, all of the lentil sauce, then the roasted eggplant, then the béchamel poured over and spread flat. Scatter the parmesan on top and bake 35 to 40 minutes, until the top is browned and set.
  6. Let it stand 20 minutes before cutting it into six squares — cut hot and it slumps. Cool fully before the lids go on.

33g protein and 31g fibre a serving. Holds six days and reheats in the microwave in 3 minutes covered, with no loss — a set bake is one of the better things to reheat. No day-of step. The lentils stay at 75g dry a serving even though the potato layer carries almost no protein, because the béchamel and the parmesan top up 9g between them. The parmesan is a topping rather than a structure: 80g across six, enough to brown. The heat here is unorthodox for a moussaka and intentional — it climbs over the week.
