Meal prep

Week of August 24

Late-August peak: charred red peppers and cherry tomatoes carry a cold lentil and bulgur salad for lunch, and a black bean chipotle mole over brown rice takes dinner while the beans are the cheapest protein in the shop. Breakfast is banana and honey overnight oats, which need no heat and use the standing bananas. The three share nothing: oats, bulgur and brown rice for grains; yogurt, lentils and black beans for protein; American, Levantine and Mexican for flavour.

Prep order
  1. Put the black beans in to soak the night before. On prep day get them simmering first and the brown rice into the rice cooker straight after — both run passively for the next hour.
  2. While they run, pour boiling water over the bulgur to steep, char the three red peppers over the flame or under the broiler, and set the lentils to simmer.
  3. Build the mole base — onion, spices, tomato, chipotle, cocoa — while the beans finish softening, then combine and season hard.
  4. Assemble last, in this order: six lunch containers with the feta and parsley scattered on top rather than mixed in, then six oat jars. Neither needs heat.
  5. Total active time: about 1h50.

Banana and Honey Overnight Oats

6 servings15 min active29g protein8g fibreAmerican

Ingredients

  • Rolled oats, 3 cups (300g)
  • Plain Greek yogurt, 3 cups (720g)
  • Protein powder, 3 scoops (about 90g) — from the bulk tub, not on the grocery list
  • Water or milk, 2 cups (480ml)
  • Honey, 6 tbsp (120g)
  • Ground cinnamon, 1 1/2 tsp
  • Bananas, 6 — sliced in at serving, from the staples bunch
  • Salt, a pinch per jar

Method

  1. Divide the oats between six lidded jars, about 1/2 cup (50g) in each.
  2. Add half a scoop of protein powder to each jar and stir it through the dry oats first — powder that goes in after the liquid clumps and stays clumped.
  3. Spoon 1/2 cup (120g) of Greek yogurt into each jar, then add 1 tbsp of honey, a pinch of cinnamon and a pinch of salt.
  4. Pour about 80ml of water or milk into each and stir hard until no dry oats are left sitting at the bottom.
  5. Lid them and refrigerate at least eight hours before the first one is eaten; they thicken as they sit.
  6. Slice half a banana over the top on the morning you eat it, never on prep day.

About 29g protein and 8g fibre per serving with the banana. Holds all 6 days sealed and is eaten cold, no reheating. Bananas must go in at serving — sliced in advance they brown and weep into the oats. If a jar has stiffened by day 5, stir in a splash of water.

Lentil, Bulgur and Charred Red Pepper Salad with Feta

6 servings40 min active28g protein15g fibreLevantine

Ingredients

  • Brown lentils, dried, 2 1/4 cups / 1 lb (454g)
  • Bulgur wheat, 2 cups / 12 oz (340g)
  • Red bell peppers, 3
  • Cherry tomatoes, 10 oz (280g), halved
  • Crumbled feta, 6 oz (170g)
  • Flat-leaf parsley, 1 bunch, chopped
  • Lemon, 1 — zest and juice
  • Vegetable oil, 5 tbsp (75ml)
  • Garlic, 2 cloves, minced
  • Ground cumin, 2 tsp
  • Smoked paprika, 1 tsp
  • Red pepper flakes, 1 1/2 tsp
  • Salt and black pepper

Method

  1. Rinse the lentils and simmer them in plenty of unsalted water for 20 to 25 minutes, until tender but still holding their shape, then drain and spread them on a tray to cool.
  2. While they cook, put the bulgur in a bowl, pour over 3 cups (720ml) of boiling water with a big pinch of salt, cover it and leave it 20 minutes, then fluff it with a fork.
  3. Char the peppers whole over a gas flame or under the broiler, turning until they are blackened all over, about 12 minutes.
  4. Seal the hot peppers in a bowl under a plate for 10 minutes so the skins steam loose, then rub the skins off, discard the stems and seeds, and cut the flesh into wide strips.
  5. Whisk the lemon zest and juice with the oil, garlic, cumin, smoked paprika, red pepper flakes and a heavy pinch of salt.
  6. Fold the cooled lentils, bulgur, pepper strips and halved tomatoes through the dressing, then taste and correct the salt and the acid — cold food needs more of both than it seems to.
  7. Divide between six containers and scatter the feta and parsley over the top of each rather than mixing them through, so they stay distinct out to day 6.

About 28g protein and 15g fibre per serving. Eaten cold, holds 6 days, and improves by day 2 as the bulgur drinks the dressing. The chili flakes climb through the week, so 1 1/2 tsp on prep day lands hot by Thursday. Walnuts were cut from this recipe to hold the budget — see the cost note.

Black Bean and Chipotle Mole with Brown Rice

6 servings55 min active29g protein15g fibreMexican

Ingredients

  • Black beans, dried, 3 1/3 cups / 1 1/2 lb (680g)
  • Brown rice, 2 1/4 cups / 1 lb (450g)
  • Yellow onions, 2 large / about 1 3/4 lb (800g), diced
  • Garlic, 4 cloves, minced
  • Jalapenos, 2, sliced with the seeds in
  • Crushed tomatoes, 15 oz (425g)
  • Chipotles in adobo, 3 peppers plus 1 tbsp of the sauce, chopped
  • Unsweetened cocoa powder, 2 tbsp (12g)
  • Ground cumin, 1 tbsp
  • Chili powder, 1 tbsp
  • Smoked paprika, 2 tsp
  • Cayenne, 1/2 tsp
  • Vegetable oil, 3 tbsp (45ml)
  • Salt
  • Plain Greek yogurt, 180g per serving, added at the table

Method

  1. Soak the black beans overnight in cold water, or cover them with boiling water for an hour if prep day has already started, then drain them.
  2. Simmer the beans in fresh unsalted water for 50 to 70 minutes until they are completely soft, topping up the water as needed, and salt them only in the last 10 minutes — salted early they stay tough.
  3. Rinse the brown rice and set it going in the rice cooker with 4 1/2 cups (1.06L) of water while the beans simmer.
  4. Fry the onion in the oil over medium heat for 10 minutes until it is browning at the edges, then add the garlic, jalapeno, cumin, chili powder, smoked paprika and cayenne and cook one minute more.
  5. Stir in the crushed tomatoes, the chopped chipotles with their adobo, and the cocoa, and simmer 10 minutes until the sauce darkens and thickens.
  6. Add the drained beans with about 1 cup (240ml) of their cooking liquid, simmer 15 minutes, then mash a ladleful against the side of the pan to thicken the whole pot and season hard with salt.
  7. Divide the rice between six containers and spoon the mole over it, leaving the yogurt off until the moment you eat.

About 29g protein and 15g fibre per serving, plus roughly 19g more from the 180g of Greek yogurt at the table. Holds 6 days. Microwave 2.5 to 3 minutes covered, stirring halfway, with a splash of water if it has stiffened, and add the yogurt after reheating, never before. The heat climbs over the week — this lands medium-hot on day 1 and properly hot by day 5, so keep hot sauce on the side if day 1 feels tame. Pepitas were cut from this recipe to hold the budget.
